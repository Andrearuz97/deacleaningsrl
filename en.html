About us

Dea Cleaning Srls was founded with the goal of offering a truly professional, reliable and transparent cleaning and sanitizing service. We believe that taking care of a space means more than simply cleaning: it means respecting the environment entrusted to us and returning it clean, orderly and ready to welcome those who use it.

We personally follow every client, from the first contact to the on-site inspection, up to defining a tailored cleaning plan. Whether it is a private home, a holiday apartment or a villa, we bring the same care, seriousness and precision to every service.

We use professional products and appropriate equipment, working methodically and carefully in every environment. Our priority is to deliver concrete results, agreed timelines and a well-organised service.

Dea Cleaning Srls works with a responsible team, focusing on service continuity and clear communication. We believe in trust, consistency and daily commitment.
