Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy describes how cookies are used on the Dea Cleaning Srls website.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device while browsing a website. They improve navigation, enable certain features and allow the provision of requested services.

Types of cookies used on this website

1. Technical cookies (necessary)

These cookies are essential for the correct functioning of the website and do not require user consent. They include:

  • session cookies
  • security cookies
  • cookies used by FormSubmit for the contact form

2. Third-party cookies

This website makes external services available only according to the user’s choice:

• Google Maps (Google Ireland Ltd.)
Used to display the company location in the “Where we are” section. The map is blocked initially: its URL remains in the data-src attribute and is assigned to the iframe only after explicit consent. Choosing “Necessary only” makes no request to Google Maps.
Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

• FormSubmit
Used to send messages through the contact form. It may use technical cookies for security.
Information: https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf

Managing cookies

The preference is saved in the browser’s localStorage. The “Manage cookies” command in the footer deletes it and displays the choice again.

Cookie banner

On the first visit, the site lets users choose between “Necessary only” and consent to Google Maps. Continuing to browse does not constitute consent.
